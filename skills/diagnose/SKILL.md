# Diagnose

Deeply analyze the available trajectory data. Reconstruct what happened and reason through the causal chain step by step until you identify the root cause. Report the reasoning and root cause to the user.

Provide a repair proposal that fixes the root cause instead of masking its symptoms. Follow the New Jersey ("Worse Is Better") style: keep the implementation and interface simple, prioritize implementation simplicity, and prefer a limited but basically correct solution over consistency, completeness, or generality that adds unnecessary complexity. Avoid overengineering.
